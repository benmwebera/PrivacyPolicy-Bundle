Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

bundle. is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What We Store

Video links, folder names, cover images, and captions you save are stored locally on your device only. We do not create user accounts or collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.

Third-Party Services

Cover images are fetched directly from TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube's servers when you save a link. These requests are made by your device directly to those platforms — we do not proxy or log them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third parties. All saved content stays on your device.

Data Deletion

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data permanently. There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of.
